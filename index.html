03Systems

Intentra

Web3 · 48-hour hackathon

I owned the backend for a four-person ETHOnline 2026 submission — and I had never written a line of Web3 before the weekend started.

Intentra ties every payment to what a person actually authorised and what was actually delivered: the AI proposes, the human signs an EIP-712 mandate, USDC sits in a Solidity escrow on the Arc testnet, delivery photos are hashed and anchored on-chain, and a disputed job settles only on a split both parties signed. I built the FastAPI modular monolith that orchestrates it — the trusted coordinator that never holds funds.

The engineering discipline is the part I'd point at. Money states commit on chain events only: the API requests, an event watcher applies them. A single state machine is the only writer of transaction state, and that invariant is enforced by an AST scan in the test suite rather than by convention. Signature verification, idempotency keys, an outbox and a reconciliation pass hold the rest together.
